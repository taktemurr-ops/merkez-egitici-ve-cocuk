Kids Learning Games brings 32 learning games together: the alphabet, letter tracing and syllables; numbers, clocks, adding, times tables, fractions and money; shapes, coloring and piano; animals, seasons, planets, flags, a map puzzle and road safety; focus games, mazes and block coding. Instructions use pictures, so even pre-readers can play.

• Letters and reading: Learn the Alphabet, Letter Tracing, Syllable Reading, English Word Cards
• Numbers and math: Learn Numbers, Adding and Taking Away, Times Tables, Math Race, Fractions, Counting Money, Tell the Time
• Shapes, art and music: Shapes and Colors, Geometry Shapes, Coloring Book, Color by Number, Kids Piano, Meet the Music Notes
• Explore the world: Animals and Sounds, Seasons and Weather, Solar System, Flags of the World, Capitals and Maps, Türkiye Map Puzzle, Road Safety
• Think and find: Spot the Odd One, Mazes and Puzzles, Block Coding, Opposites, Left, Right and Where
• Me and my world: Feelings, Healthy Habits, Recycling Sort

Highlights
• Difficulty adapts to your child: fewer choices when it gets hard, new steps after each success.
• Daily and weekly levels, missions, achievements and 33 languages.
• A sticker book, a parent report, a daily time limit and bedtime; all data stays on the device.
• Plays offline on phones, tablets and the web.
• Designed for children: no advertising ID or analytics; purchases and settings sit behind a parental gate.
• No gambling, betting or 18+ content; suitable for all ages.

Purchases (such as removing ads) apply to every game in the app.

Built for the App Store Kids category: no interstitial ads; any ads shown are non-personalized and child-directed.
